# Probability and Quality Review

Before returning JSON, check: correct player/team and latest credible availability; current-season stats and recent role/sample context; explicitly assess all researched QB/RB/WR/TE players and injury beneficiaries for player props; aim for 4–5 strong picks with 2–3 player props when evidence supports them, but never fabricate to fill slots; never duplicate same-team moneyline and spread; a spread probability cannot exceed that team's moneyline probability; every claimed line has supplied evidence or is exactly "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets, not offers; probabilities are conservative and distinct from confidence; rationale and risks are measurable; injury impacts are evidence-supported and separate from picks. For TD candidates, require current availability and dated evidence; questionable is conditional, max confidence 6/10 with explicit risk; exclude doubtful, out, and injured-reserve players. Up to five bets and three TD candidates may be returned; shorter or empty lists are valid when evidence is insufficient. Never fabricate evidence. Reject invented stats, injuries, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations, and backtests. Lower confidence/data quality and disclose gaps when evidence is weak.
