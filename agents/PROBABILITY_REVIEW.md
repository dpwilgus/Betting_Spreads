# Probability and Quality Review

Review player/team identity, current availability, recent usage and sample size. Assess every researched offensive player, defensive starter/leader, kicker, punter and injury beneficiary across all evidence-supported prop types and full-game/first-quarter/first-half game markets. Target five verified player props only when sportsbook line and price evidence exists; model-only thresholds must be separated from actionable sportsbook picks. Never invent a line. Keep game bets separate; avoid overlapping same-team moneyline/spread picks. Verify every line or label it "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets. Keep probability distinct from confidence. TDs need current availability and scoring/opportunity evidence; QBs need 3+ rushing TDs, or 2+ rushing TDs plus 3+ carries/game (passing TDs do not count). Questionable is conditional (max 6/10 with explicit risk); exclude Doubtful, Out and IR. Never invent evidence, stats, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations or backtests; disclose gaps and lower confidence when evidence is weak.

- Review every researched offensive candidate, defensive starter/leader, kicker and punter, plus next-man-up candidates added for relevant injuries. Check attempts, completions, TDs, interceptions, receptions, targets, longest reception, tackles, sacks, defensive takeaways, field goals and other stats actually supplied. Do not omit a high-profile starter merely because the AI did not select that player in its initial picks.
- Reject self-referential injury impacts: the subject cannot be their own affected player. Player beneficiaries must be on a matchup roster, available and supported by structured ESPN game-log evidence; "Team unit" may be used when a material starter absence has no clear individual beneficiary. Verify news/role-change impacts against supplied team-news headlines and dates. Injury replacements are candidates to assess, not automatic positive beneficiaries.
- The matchup summary must use recent team-news headlines and compare the two defenses with available defensive efficiency, pressure, sack, takeaway and recent-game statistics. Identify missing metrics instead of making unsupported claims.
