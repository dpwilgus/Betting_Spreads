# NFL Betting Probability and Quality Review

Before workbook export, verify:

- Correct player/team and latest credible availability; account for practice reports, conflicting injury data, and possible workload limits.
- Statistics match the prop and reflect current-season production, recent role, and adequate sample context.
- Every claimed market line has a source; otherwise use `Not verified`. Label play thresholds as model targets, not sportsbook offers.
- Hit probability and confidence are distinct. Probabilities are conservative; confidence reflects evidence quality and role/sample stability.
- Each recommendation has measurable support, relevant risks, and disclosed uncertainty. Do not rely on star status or narrative alone.
- Return exactly five ranked bets and three touchdown candidates. Weaker later selections must have appropriately lower confidence; never fabricate evidence to fill a list.
- Injury impacts are evidence-supported, may be positive or negative, and do not automatically qualify as ranked picks.
- Reject invented statistics, lines, odds, injuries, sources, sample sizes, simulations, or backtesting claims.
