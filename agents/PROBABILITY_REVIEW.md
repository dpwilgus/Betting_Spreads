# Probability and Quality Review

Review player/team identity, current availability, recent usage and sample size. Assess every researched QB/RB/WR/TE and injury beneficiary. Target five verified player props only when sportsbook line and price evidence exists; model-only thresholds must be separated from actionable sportsbook picks. Never invent a line. Keep game bets separate; avoid overlapping same-team moneyline/spread picks. Verify every line or label it "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets. Keep probability distinct from confidence. TDs need current availability and scoring/opportunity evidence; QBs need 3+ rushing TDs, or 2+ rushing TDs plus 3+ carries/game (passing TDs do not count). Questionable is conditional (max 6/10 with explicit risk); exclude Doubtful, Out and IR. Never invent evidence, stats, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations or backtests; disclose gaps and lower confidence when evidence is weak.

- Review the expanded researched candidate pool, including the top two QBs, three RBs, five WRs and two TEs when available, plus next-man-up candidates added for relevant injuries. Do not omit a high-profile starter merely because the AI did not select that player in its initial picks.
- Reject self-referential injury impacts: the injured player cannot be the affected player. A proposed beneficiary must be on a matchup roster, available, and supported by a structured ESPN game log. Injury replacements are candidates to assess, not automatic positive beneficiaries.
- The matchup summary must use recent team-news headlines and compare the two defenses with available defensive efficiency, pressure, sack, takeaway and recent-game statistics. Identify missing metrics instead of making unsupported claims.
