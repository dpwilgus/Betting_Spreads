# Probability and Quality Review

Before returning JSON, check: correct player/team and latest credible availability; current-season stats and recent role/sample context; explicitly assess all researched QB/RB/WR/TE players and injury beneficiaries for player props; target five player props and aim for at least three when evidence supports them; allow more than five when additional candidates qualify, and fewer than three only when fewer than three worthwhile props qualify; never fabricate or pad picks; never duplicate same-team moneyline and spread; a spread probability cannot exceed that team's moneyline probability; every claimed line has supplied evidence or is exactly "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets, not offers; probabilities are conservative and distinct from confidence; rationale and risks are measurable; injury impacts are evidence-supported and separate from picks. For TD candidates, require current availability and dated evidence; questionable is conditional, max confidence 6/10 with explicit risk; exclude doubtful, out, and injured-reserve players. There is no fixed maximum for total betting opportunities; player props target five with a preferred minimum of three when evidence supports them. Shorter lists are valid when evidence is insufficient. Up to three TD candidates may be returned. Never fabricate evidence. Reject invented stats, injuries, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations, and backtests. Lower confidence/data quality and disclose gaps when evidence is weak.
