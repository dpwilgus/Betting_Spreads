# Probability and Quality Review

Review player/team identity, current availability, recent usage and sample size. Assess researched offensive players for evidence-supported props and evaluate defensive leaders' sacks/tackles only as inputs to defensive matchup analytics. Do not recommend defensive sacks/tackles, kicking points, field goals, kicker or punter props. Assess full-game/first-quarter/first-half game markets when evidence and quotes support them. Target five verified player props only when sportsbook line and price evidence exists; model-only thresholds must be separated from actionable sportsbook picks. Never invent a line. Keep game bets separate; avoid overlapping same-team moneyline/spread picks. Assess underdog cover and upset chances in every spread game; only recommend a priced edge, never force a dog bet. Verify every line or label it "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets. Keep probability distinct from confidence. TDs need current availability and scoring/opportunity evidence; QBs need 3+ rushing TDs, or 2+ rushing TDs plus 3+ carries/game (passing TDs do not count). Questionable is conditional (max 6/10 with explicit risk); exclude Doubtful, Out and IR. Never invent evidence, stats, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations or backtests; disclose gaps and lower confidence when evidence is weak.

- Review every researched offensive candidate, defensive starter/leader, kicker and punter, plus next-man-up candidates added for relevant injuries. Check attempts, completions, TDs (including anytime TD), interceptions, receptions, targets, longest reception and other supported offensive markets. Use tackles, sacks and defensive takeaways only to assess opposing offense matchups; do not make them betting recommendations. Do not omit a high-profile starter merely because the AI did not select that player in its initial picks.
- Reject self-referential injury impacts: the subject cannot be their own affected player. Player beneficiaries must be on a matchup roster, available and supported by structured ESPN game-log evidence; "Team unit" may be used when a material starter absence has no clear individual beneficiary. Verify news/role-change impacts against supplied team-news headlines and dates. Injury replacements are candidates to assess, not automatic positive beneficiaries.
- The matchup summary must use dated team news and compare pass/rush/points defense separately. Sacks alone do not make a defense elite; identify missing metrics. Verify neutral venues and QB experience/former-team ties; heritage is context, not a performance edge by itself.
