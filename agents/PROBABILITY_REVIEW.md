# Probability and Quality Review

Before returning JSON, check: correct player/team and latest credible availability; current-season stats and recent role/sample context; every claimed line has supplied evidence or is exactly "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets, not offers; probabilities are conservative and distinct from confidence; rationale and risks are measurable; injury impacts are evidence-supported and separate from picks. Exactly five bets and three TD candidates are required, but never fabricate evidence. Reject invented stats, injuries, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations, and backtests. Lower confidence/data quality and disclose gaps when evidence is weak.
