# Probability and Quality Review

Review player/team identity, current availability, recent usage and sample size. Assess every researched QB/RB/WR/TE and injury beneficiary. Target five player props; aim for at least three when supported, allow more than five when justified, and return fewer than three only if fewer than three worthwhile props qualify. Never pad. Keep game bets separate; avoid overlapping same-team moneyline/spread picks. Verify every line or label it "Not verified"; thresholds are model targets. Keep probability distinct from confidence. TD candidates need current dated availability and demonstrated position-specific scoring/opportunity (TD totals, carries/targets per game, and recent scoring); a QB needs at least one rushing TD or 3+ rushing attempts per game because passing TDs are not his anytime-TD score. Questionable is conditional (max 6/10 with explicit risk); exclude Doubtful, Out and IR. Never invent evidence, stats, lines, odds, sources, samples, simulations or backtests; disclose gaps and lower confidence when evidence is weak.
